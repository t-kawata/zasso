## Summary

Completed the M3 milestone: migrated the `AsrBackend` trait from voiput's internal definition to the external `trate` crate. This completes Phase 1 and Phase 2 of the trate/Qwen3-ASR initiative, covering all 15 tickets across M0-M3 milestones.

- **docs: update Tickets.md** — marked M3-1 through M3-5 as completed
