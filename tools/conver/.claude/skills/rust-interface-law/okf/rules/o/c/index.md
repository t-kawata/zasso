- [SSO-09 — Accounts MUST NOT be linked automatically by email address. Linking requires an explicit, auditable action.](./sso-09.md)
- [SSO-10 — Provider client IDs and secrets MUST come from configuration or environment variables, never source code.](./sso-10.md)
- [SSO-11 — Adding SSO MUST change only: the `identity-sso` crate, authentication adapters, assembly wiring, configuration, and `external_identities`. Business operations, `VerifiedIdentity`, and business tables MUST remain unchanged.](./sso-11.md)
